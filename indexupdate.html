💡 Good to know

This app reads books you have permission to use. It uses your phone’s built-in text-to-speech voices, so voices can vary between devices.

On iPhone, tap Play to start sound. Voice playback may stop when the screen locks or you switch apps, depending on iOS and browser behavior.
